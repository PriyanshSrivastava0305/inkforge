Twelve stages in three movements. Many stories reorder or skip some; keep the transformation.

**Departure**
1. **Ordinary world**: who the hero is before, and what's missing.
2. **Call to adventure**: a problem or invitation arrives.
3. **Refusal**: fear or duty makes the hero hesitate.
4. **Meeting the mentor**: guidance, a gift or confidence.
5. **Crossing the threshold**: the hero commits and enters an unfamiliar world.

**Initiation**
6. **Tests, allies, enemies**: learning the new world's rules; loyalties form.
7. **Approach**: preparing for the central danger.
8. **Ordeal**: a confrontation with the greatest fear; a symbolic death.
9. **Reward**: something gained (an object, knowledge, reconciliation).

**Return**
10. **The road back**: consequences chase the hero; commitment to finish.
11. **Resurrection**: a final test where the hero must use everything they've learned. The old self dies for good.
12. **Return with the elixir**: the hero comes home changed, bringing something that heals the ordinary world.

Checks: the ordeal sits near the middle, not the end. The resurrection is a *different and harder* test than the ordeal. What the hero brings back answers what was missing in stage 1.
