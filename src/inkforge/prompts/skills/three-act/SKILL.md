title: Three-act structure
kind: structure
version: 1
description: Setup, confrontation, resolution, with two hard turning points.
stages: [design, outline]
beats: [setup, inciting incident, first turning point, rising complications, midpoint, crisis, climax, resolution]
---
Shape the story in three movements of roughly 25% / 50% / 25% of the length.

**Act 1 — Setup (≈0–25%).** Show the protagonist's ordinary world and what they lack. An inciting incident disturbs it (around 10–12%). The act ends on a *first turning point*: the protagonist commits to a goal and can't go back.

**Act 2 — Confrontation (≈25–75%).** Obstacles escalate, and each attempt costs more than the last. At the *midpoint* (≈50%) a revelation or reversal changes what the goal means: a false victory or a false defeat. Pressure builds to a *crisis* (≈75%) where the old approach fails completely.

**Act 3 — Resolution (≈75–100%).** The protagonist makes a choice that shows who they've become, and acts on it at the *climax*. Answer the dramatic question clearly. A short resolution shows the new normal.

Checks: every act ends on a turn that changes direction, not just intensity. The climax is caused by the protagonist's choice, not by luck. Every setup in Act 1 that matters is paid off by the end of Act 3.
