Fifteen beats. Positions are a share of total length; treat them as targets, not laws.

1. **Opening image (0–1%)**: a snapshot of the "before" state.
2. **Theme stated (≈5%)**: someone hints at the lesson the hero will need, and the hero doesn't get it yet.
3. **Set-up (1–10%)**: the hero's world, flaws and stakes; what needs fixing.
4. **Catalyst (≈10%)**: the event that knocks the hero's life off course.
5. **Debate (10–20%)**: hesitation; should they go?
6. **Break into two (≈20%)**: the hero chooses to enter the new situation.
7. **B story (≈22%)**: a relationship that carries the theme.
8. **Fun and games (20–50%)**: the premise is delivered; the hero explores the new world.
9. **Midpoint (≈50%)**: a false victory or false defeat; stakes rise and the clock starts.
10. **Bad guys close in (50–75%)**: external pressure and internal doubt both tighten.
11. **All is lost (≈75%)**: the lowest point; something or someone is lost.
12. **Dark night of the soul (75–80%)**: the hero processes the loss and finally understands the theme.
13. **Break into three (≈80%)**: a new plan that combines A and B stories.
14. **Finale (80–99%)**: the plan is executed, goes wrong, and the hero adapts using what they learned.
15. **Final image (99–100%)**: the "after" picture, the opposite of the opening image.

Checks: the catalyst happens *to* the hero; the break into two is chosen *by* the hero. The midpoint and all-is-lost mirror each other (high/low or low/high).
